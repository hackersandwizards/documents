hackers&wizards Dokumente: Muster

Jeder Dokumenttyp aus @hackersandwizards/documents, mit erfundenen Daten. Jeder Betrag ist ein Platzhalter. Das Gate baut diese Seiten neu und vergleicht sie.

Aufbau

Drei Ebenen. Tokens: tokens.css gibt jedem Wert eine Rolle (Farbe, Schrift, Abstand, Seite) aus @hackersandwizards/brand. Basis: document.css setzt Seite, Laufkopf und die Elemente, die jeder Typ und jeder Markdown-Text nutzt (Überschriften, Tabellen, Label, Callout, Karten). Komponenten setzen sich nur aus Tokens und Basis zusammen, Typen nur aus Komponenten. Jeder Typ beginnt mit DocHeader; das Gate documents-styles lehnt jeden Stil außerhalb dieser Ebenen ab.

Typen

TypZweckMuster
DocProposalThe proposal (Angebot), German or English: the lead, the price on page 1, about us, scope, the optional sequence, the outcome, terms, closing and acceptance, in that fixed order.Angebot, Quote (English), Angebot ohne Preise
DocRateCardThe personal rate card, composed from the document components.Honorarsätze
DocLeistungsnachweisThe Leistungsnachweis, one template for both modes, a month and a training engagement: the header, the meta box with its fields in one order, the hours table with its total, and the client's confirmation.Leistungsnachweis, ein Monat, Leistungsnachweis, ein Training in Abschnitten, Leistungsnachweis, pauschal
DocAgreementAn agreement signed by hand, German or English: a contract, an NDA, a framework agreement, a statement of work, a change order or an order confirmation.Vereinbarung: Geheimhaltung (Vertrag), Vereinbarung: Auftragsbestätigung (Brief)
DocCertificateThe training certificate: a fixed one-page layout on the document base, no page margin, counter, body padding or paragraph spacing.Certificate (English), Zertifikat
DocOnepagerThe training-offering onepager: DocHeader with the website's address as its linked tag, then the body.Onepager
DocHandoutThe printed training handout: a cover with the header, the title, an optional recipient card and the contents, then one section per chapter, each opening on its title.Training Handout (English)
DocPreparationThe preparation document a trainer sends attendees before a training: the header, the eyebrow and title, a note with what to do by when, numbered steps as cards, a closing line.Vorbereitung
DocMarkdownA Markdown document (print-pdf, the delivery and trainer briefings, a report): the header, then the body as DocProse.Delivery Briefing, Trainer Briefing (English, with meta), Dokument aus Markdown
composeSurveyReadoutDie Auswertung einer Teilnehmerumfrage als Folien (1280 × 720), auf denselben Tokens.Survey Readout

Komponenten

KomponenteZweckGenutzt von
DocCalloutThe outcome callout: InShort.astro's accent rule on the left of a tint with the brand corner on all four corners, here in purple, which marks the result and nothing else (EventRow.astro's next event; the heading 4.9:1 on the tint).DocProposal
DocTotalRowThe total row of a table: Price.astro's card amount in the h4 role on the teal band, a 12% tint under a strong teal rule, each figure in teal at 1.2 times.DocProposal, DocRateCard, DocLeistungsnachweis
DocClosingThe closing, kept whole so the sign-off never lands alone on a page: the closing text, the sign-off, then the sender's name.DocProposal, DocRateCard
DocLeadThe lead: the reader's situation in prose left, the slot "aside" (a DocRecipient) right.DocProposal, DocRateCard, DocAgreement
DocProseProse: the element rules for Markdown bodies (pandoc and Astro's markdown output) and other free text, the print twins of the website's typography: headings in mono; tables, quotes, code and links are the brand's elements (composePage maps them); lists take the document base's own rules.DocAgreement, DocHandout, DocMarkdown
DocTitleA document's title block: the eyebrow (the website's label-caps), the h1, and an optional lead (SectionHeader.astro's lead role in muted ink).DocProposal, DocRateCard, DocLeistungsnachweis, DocAgreement, DocHandout, DocPreparation
DocSectionHeadA section's head, kept with what follows: an optional section label (TrainingList.astro's group title, the h4 role's size in mono 600 capitals after a teal square), then the h2.DocProposal
DocCardThe brand's card (hw-card, which composePage adds).DocProposal, DocRateCard
DocStepsA sequence of dated steps: ruled rows (patterns.css .ruled-rows), the date in the h1 family's weight in purple, the step's name and one muted line beside it.DocProposal
DocRecipientThe recipient card, the base's .prepared on Card.astro's tint surface: a label in caps, the company bold, then the person and the address, one per line.DocProposal, DocRateCard, DocAgreement, DocHandout
DocSignaturesThe signature block of a document signed by hand: one column per party, its role in caps and its name, then a line for place and date and a line for the signature, each with room above it for the pen.DocAgreement
DocHeaderThe document header, the twin of the website's Nav.astro: a bar in the page tint across the paper, the gradient strip on top, a rule under it, the lockup left and the document tag right.DocProposal, DocRateCard, DocLeistungsnachweis, DocAgreement, DocOnepager, DocHandout, DocPreparation, DocMarkdown
DocPriceTableA price table on the base's ruled table: each row an item in bold with an optional muted detail line, then its cells; a column marked num holds figures, right-aligned in tabular digits.DocProposal, DocRateCard
DocConfirmationThe client's confirmation, kept whole on one page: a label in caps leading the statement, then the line the confirmation fills.DocLeistungsnachweis
